Our farm

Small, local and family-run.

Lahman Family Farm started in 2024 as a family poultry farm right here in New Lebanon, west of Dayton in Montgomery County.

We keep things simple: care for the birds, take care of the land, and be good neighbors. Farm days are Wednesday and Thursday. Anytime else, a quick call or text is the best way to reach us.

Questions

Good to know.

When are you open?

Wednesday and Thursday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We're closed to visitors the rest of the week, but you can call or text (937) 564-9351 any day.

Where is the farm?

860 S Church St, New Lebanon, Ohio 45345, west of Dayton in Montgomery County. Open directions in Google Maps.

How do I know what's available?

Call or text before you come. What's on hand depends on the flock and the season, so we're happy to check for you.

Can I set something aside?

Yes. Ask ahead and tell us which farm day you're coming, and we'll let you know what we can hold.

Can I email instead?

Sure. Write to erinjelizabeth.ej@gmail.com. For quick questions, a call or text usually gets the fastest answer.
